Common Questions

How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking at least 2–3 weeks in advance for most events. For weddings or large corporate events, 4–6 weeks is ideal to ensure availability and custom menu planning.
What's the minimum guest count? +
Our minimum is 20 guests. For smaller gatherings, we recommend placing a large pickup or delivery order instead.
Do you provide serving equipment? +
Yes — we bring chafing dishes, tongs, serving spoons, plates, and napkins. We can also provide an on-site taquero for a live cooking experience at an additional cost.
Can you accommodate dietary restrictions? +
Absolutely. We offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. Just let us know in your request and we'll customize the menu accordingly.
What's included in the base price? +
All catering packages include setup, food service, basic cleanup, and transportation within the Dallas metro area. Travel surcharges may apply outside 30 miles.

Our Story

Rooted in Two Cities

Donde Los Tacos was born from a familiar kind of hunger — not just for good food, but for the feeling that goes with it. The kind you get standing at a street taco stand in Colonia Roma at 1am, watching the taquero work the trompo with quiet confidence.

Our founders grew up between Dallas and Mexico City. They watched their grandmothers press tortillas the same way every morning. They spent years missing that specific taste — that combination of handmade corn masa, slow-braised meat, and a salsa that bites back just right.

"We didn't open a restaurant. We opened the place we always wished existed in our own neighborhood."

Donde Los Tacos opened on West Davis Street because Oak Cliff felt like home — a neighborhood with roots, character, and people who care about where their food comes from. Bishop Arts wasn't just a location. It was a community we already belonged to.

Every detail at Donde reflects that origin story: the masa ground fresh, the birria braised through the night, the salsas made in small batches from dried chiles and tomatillos. Nothing outsourced. Nothing frozen. Just the real thing, made with care.
